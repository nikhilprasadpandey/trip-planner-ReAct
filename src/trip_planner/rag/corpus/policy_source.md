## §1 Purpose and Scope
<!-- job_levels: ic, manager, director -->
This policy governs air travel booked for business purposes. It applies to
all employees requesting company-paid air travel, regardless of job level.
Job level determines cabin-class eligibility, spend caps, pre-approval
thresholds, and advance-booking requirements, as set out below.

## §2a Cabin Class Eligibility — Individual Contributor
<!-- job_levels: ic -->
Individual contributors must book Economy class for both domestic and
international travel. Upgrades to Premium Economy or above require prior
written approval from a Manager and are not covered by default.

## §2b Cabin Class Eligibility — Manager
<!-- job_levels: manager -->
Managers may book up to Premium Economy for domestic and international
travel. Business class requires prior approval from a Director.

## §2c Cabin Class Eligibility — Director
<!-- job_levels: director -->
Directors may book up to Business class for domestic and international
travel. First class requires prior approval from VP Finance.

## §3a Spend Caps — Individual Contributor
<!-- job_levels: ic -->
The per-ticket spend cap for an Individual Contributor is $600 for domestic
routes and $1,800 for international routes. Any fare exceeding these caps
requires pre-approval from the employee's Manager before booking.

## §3b Spend Caps — Manager
<!-- job_levels: manager -->
The per-ticket spend cap for a Manager is $1,200 for domestic routes and
$3,500 for international routes. Any fare exceeding these caps requires
pre-approval from a Director before booking.

## §3c Spend Caps — Director
<!-- job_levels: director -->
The per-ticket spend cap for a Director is $2,500 for domestic routes and
$6,000 for international routes. Any fare exceeding these caps requires
pre-approval from VP Finance before booking.

## §4 Preferred Carriers
<!-- job_levels: ic, manager, director -->
Employees at every job level should prefer American Airlines (AA), Delta
Air Lines (DL), or United Airlines (UA) when fare and schedule are
comparable to a non-preferred carrier. Booking a non-preferred carrier is
permitted but should be justified in the trip notes when the fare
difference exceeds 15%.

## §5a Advance Booking Requirements — Individual Contributor
<!-- job_levels: ic -->
Individual contributors must book domestic flights at least 7 days in
advance of departure wherever the business need allows. Late bookings
require Manager approval regardless of fare.

## §5b Advance Booking Requirements — Manager
<!-- job_levels: manager -->
Managers must book domestic flights at least 3 days in advance of
departure wherever the business need allows.

## §5c Advance Booking Requirements — Director
<!-- job_levels: director -->
Directors have no minimum advance-booking requirement, reflecting the
frequently short-notice nature of director-level travel.

## §6 Approval Routing
<!-- job_levels: ic, manager, director -->
An out-of-policy fare (over the applicable spend cap or cabin class) is
routed for approval as follows: an Individual Contributor's request routes
to their Manager; a Manager's request routes to a Director; a Director's
request routes to VP Finance. No booking action may be taken until the
routed approver has granted approval.
